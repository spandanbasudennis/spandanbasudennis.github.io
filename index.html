My research interests center on intelligent autonomous systems, with a particular focus on developing autonomous agents that can perceive, understand, learn, reason, and safely interact with the real world. I explore this through Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, and multimodal learning, with specific interests in autonomous driving and vehicle navigation, Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, intelligent decision-making, and the security, safety, and reliability of autonomous systems. I am also interested in time-series learning and applying intelligent systems to complex, multidisciplinary real-world problems.

My goal is to contribute to the development of autonomous systems that are not only capable and adaptive, but also robust, trustworthy, secure, and reliable in real-world environments. I am particularly fascinated by the challenge of bridging the gap between intelligent models and physical environments—enabling autonomous agents to learn from complex data, understand their surroundings, make informed decisions, and act effectively under uncertainty.

Alongside specific research problems, I aspire to work in a forward-thinking and collaborative research environment where autonomous systems can be explored from different perspectives and disciplines. I want to continuously grow as a researcher while contributing to research that advances intelligent autonomy and creates meaningful impact in the real world. I see research as a journey of mutual growth, where the challenges I pursue, the people I collaborate with, and the knowledge I gain shape me both professionally and personally.

Extracurricular Activities

President

Astronomy & Science Society of RUET · Executive Committee 2024–25

As President of the Astronomy & Science Society of RUET, I led a range of scientific and educational events. I organized three online sessions—two on astronomy and one on mathematics—and arranged the 16th IAU-Abdul Jabbar Astronomy Workshop. I also organized a School Camp at Subil High School in Bogura to encourage a passion for science among young students. Other initiatives included a movie session featuring “Predestination”, a Rubik’s Cube contest called “The Cube Clash”, and the Albert Einstein Memorial Pi Day Expedition 4.0.

Publication Secretary

Astronomy & Science Society of RUET · Executive Committee 2023–24

I served as one of the Chief Content Writers for ASSR, producing content for the society’s social media platforms on topics such as the Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2022, the Perseverance rover, and the James Webb Telescope. I was also a team member in organizing the Fresher’s Reception Program 2022, Protostar-2023, and the Einstein’s Birthday and Pi Day celebrations in 2022, and served as the online spokesperson for Aurora, the first National Science Festival organized by any club at RUET, held in 2023.

About Me

I am someone who finds happiness in little things, gets lost in stories, and is always curious about the world around me. I have a soft spot for creativity, imagination, and the things that make life a little more interesting. Whether it is listening to a favourite song, watching a good movie, enjoying a football match, or getting lost in a book, I believe there is something special about finding joy in the things we love.

Music has always been one of my favourite companions, especially classic Bengali and Hindi songs, whose timeless melodies and lyrics keep drawing me back. I also love watching movies and web series, with comedy, thriller, and romantic comedy being my favourite genres. Beyond music and movies, I enjoy playing cricket and football, though football holds a special place in my heart. I am a hardcore supporter of the Brazil National Football Team and FC Barcelona. For me, football is more than just a sport; it is a blend of passion, excitement, and emotions that makes every match memorable.

But if there is one thing that can truly distract me from everything else, it is books. I have a special weakness for them, particularly science fiction and detective stories. My all-time favourite fictional character is Prodosh Chandra Mitter (ABCD by Mr. L. Ganguly), better known as Feluda, the brilliant private investigator created by the great Satyajit Ray. I am also a great admirer of the writings Humayun Ahmed and Dr. Muhammad Zafar Iqbal, whose writing holds a special place in my heart.

End of the day, I appreciate stories that make me think, songs that stir emotions, games that keep me on the edge of my seat, and moments that inspire new ideas. There is still so much I want to read, watch, experience, and create, and I hope I never lose that sense of curiosity.

Thank you for visiting. Have a wonderful day.
